Before the get-together

How we actually use AI

Nine questions, about five minutes. Richard Barry is running the AI sessions, and this tells him where we each are so he doesn't spend two days on things half of us already know and the other half can't follow.

This is anonymous. No sign-in, no email address collected, and nothing asking your role. The name box at the end is optional.

There are no wrong answers and this isn't a test of anyone. "I haven't really used it" is a genuinely useful answer and several of us will be giving it.
